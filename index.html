Manufacturing Data Platform & Defect Risk Intelligence

Deliverables for a sheet-metal fabrication shop, from integrated data to a live, embedded defect-risk model.

Six deliverables trace the work end to end: a diagnosis of where defects and scrap cost concentrate, a machine learning model that scores each work order's defect risk before it runs, that score delivered inside the existing work-order system, and the MLOps monitoring that keeps it honest over time. Full detail is in the README.
